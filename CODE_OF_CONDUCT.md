# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and keep discussions about
the work. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or
the language they write in.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing
someone's private information, and sexual language or imagery.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, tell the maintainer privately through
[GitHub's report feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or the contact on the maintainer's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/codewithelvin). Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, code or issues that break
this code, and may block people who keep doing so.
